Atlas of Wisdom
Product Development Plan (Version 1.0)
Vision

Atlas of Wisdom is an interactive educational platform that allows users to explore the world's spiritual, philosophical, and cultural heritage through geography, history, and storytelling.

It is not simply a map of places, but an atlas of ideas—showing where wisdom traditions originated, how they spread across civilisations, and how influential people, sacred places, and timeless teachings shaped humanity.

Users become explorers, historians, and collectors, embarking on a lifelong journey of discovery.

Tagline

Discover the places that shaped humanity's wisdom.

Mission

To make the world's spiritual and philosophical heritage accessible, engaging, and enjoyable for everyone by combining interactive maps, historical storytelling, gamification, and exploration.

Product Philosophy

Atlas of Wisdom is founded on four principles:

Explore — Curiosity begins with discovery.
Understand — Learn the historical and cultural significance of places and people.
Connect — Discover relationships between ideas, traditions, and civilisations.
Preserve — Appreciate humanity's shared cultural heritage.

Unlike a travel guide, Atlas of Wisdom encourages intellectual exploration rather than physical tourism.

Future Expansion
Pilgrimage Mode

Virtually accompany great historical journeys, unlocking locations and stories in sequence. Examples include the journeys of Confucius, Xuanzang, the Buddha, Paul, and the Camino de Santiago.

Architecture Explorer

Study architectural styles, symbols, and construction across cultures through interactive models and recognition games.

UNESCO Heritage Explorer

A dedicated mode for exploring UNESCO-recognised cultural and spiritual heritage, with regional challenges and curated collections.

Cultural Festivals Calendar

An annual calendar highlighting major cultural and religious festivals, their origins, associated places, customs, and historical significance.

Before & After Explorer

Compare archaeological reconstructions, historical illustrations, and present-day photographs using interactive sliders to understand how important sites have changed through time.

Story Trails

Follow themed routes connecting multiple places into a narrative, such as:

Birth of Buddhism
Spread of Confucianism
Ancient Centres of Learning
Sacred Mountains of Asia
The Silk Road
Mystery Explorer

A daily puzzle mode where users solve location-based mysteries using historical clues, maps, and architectural details.

Time Traveller

Select any historical year and experience the world as it existed then, revealing contemporary civilisations, active historical figures, and significant sites.

Living Connections

An ever-expanding interactive network that reveals relationships among people, places, texts, virtues, and historical events, allowing users to explore knowledge organically rather than linearly.

Long-Term Vision

Atlas of Wisdom seeks to become the world's most engaging interactive atlas of humanity's intellectual, philosophical, and spiritual heritage. Rather than presenting isolated facts, it invites users to journey through the interconnected stories of people, places, ideas, and cultures.

By combining geography, history, exploration, and gamification, it transforms learning into an ongoing adventure. Users do not simply read about history—they follow the footsteps of remarkable individuals, uncover forgotten places, solve exploration challenges, build collections, and gradually develop a richer understanding of the shared heritage of humanity.

The central message is:

Every place tells a story. Every story carries wisdom. Every journey deepens understanding.
